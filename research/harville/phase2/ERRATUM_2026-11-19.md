# Erratum to Phase 2 (added 19 Nov 2026 during Phase 3; the original files are unchanged)

**What Phase 2 said:** DISAGREEMENT_ANALYSIS.md §4 and FINAL_REPORT.md answer 3 said "the market moves away from our model". The evidence was a -0.17 / -0.27 correlation between log(our chance / 09:00 market) and the later move to BSP.

**Correction:** that correlation is mostly an odds-level artefact.
- Our model is much flatter than the market: it rates outsiders above the market and favourites below it.
- Outsiders also lengthen systematically between the 09:00 composite price and BSP.
- **Compared within the same 09:00 odds band** (20 equal bands), the correlation is **positive**:
  - +0.14 (May-Aug);
  - +0.05 (Sep-Oct);
  - +0.05 (26 Oct - 18 Nov, untouched).
- So, at the same odds, the market tends to move **towards** our model's view, mainly by drifting the horses we rate below the market.

See research/market_intelligence/phase3/MARKET_MOVEMENT.md.

**What is not affected:** Phase 2's place-betting conclusions (no edge at place SP) and its accuracy results.
